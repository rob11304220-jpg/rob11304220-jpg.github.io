About

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Science and Technology of China. I believe mathematical methods can enable more precise and efficient translation between 3D models and the physical world. Currently, I am working on surface reconstruction from B-Rep data. My focus is on inferring the designer's intent from existing parametric models to produce high-quality, watertight surfaces.
